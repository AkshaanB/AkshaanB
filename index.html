About Me

I am an AI researcher with over 3 years of industry experience specializing in Artificial Intelligence and Computer Vision, along with 4+ years of expertise in Software Development. My passion for leveraging AI to solve real-world problems has driven me to explore the intersection of technology and human-centered solutions.

Currently, I am engaged in research focused on Medical Imaging and Assistive Technologies, where I apply AI and Computer Vision to develop cutting-edge solutions that enhance healthcare diagnostics and empower individuals with disabilities. My work aims to push the boundaries of what AI can achieve in these fields, delivering impactful innovations.

I hold a First Class Honors degree in BEng Software Engineering from the University of Westminster, which laid the foundation for my work in both technology and research.
